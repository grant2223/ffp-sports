Questions

Gear rental

How does rental work?

Pick your gear and dates, then book. Collect it free from us in Dubai, or we drop it off and pick it up. Each is a separate charge: AED 150 in Dubai, 225 in Sharjah and Ajman, 375 in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain, 450 in the northern emirates.

Is there a deposit?

Yes, AED 2,000 per kit or AED 500 per camera or similar item, paid with the rental and refunded when the gear comes back undamaged. Tripods and headsets need no deposit.

What do I need to rent?

Your Emirates ID or passport, uploaded when you book.

Can someone show us how it works?

Add Set-up and handover. An FFP operator sets everything up at your venue and shows your team how to run it.

Can you run it for us instead?

Yes. Book a livestream and our crew brings and runs the gear.

Do you give a discount for longer rentals?

Yes. 10% off for 3 to 6 days, and 20% off for 7 days or more.

What if something breaks?

Tell us straight away. Repairs come out of the deposit, up to the repair cost.

Can I extend my rental?

Yes, if the gear is free. Message us before your return date.

Is the vMix system hard to run?

It needs an experienced operator. Most clients add Set-up and handover, or book our crew to run it.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
