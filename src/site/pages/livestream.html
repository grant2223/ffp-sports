Questions

Livestream

Which package do I need?

1 camera suits club matches and single courts. 3 cameras suits finals, tournaments and national events, and anything with commentary.

Can we book the whole season?

Yes. Choose Season when you book: 10 matches, same crew, and 15% off every match.

Is it a national or international match?

Book Full day. National and international matches need the full set-up and run time.

Where does the stream go?

YouTube, Facebook and your website, all at once. We can stream to your own channel: add us as a manager or send your stream key.

Can the stream be private?

Yes. We can stream unlisted, so only people with the link can watch.

Do we need internet at the venue?

No. 4G internet is built into our kit. If the venue has a cable line, we use it as a backup.

What do we need to have ready?

A power point within 20 metres of the camera spot, and access for our crew 60 minutes before start.

How do we get player graphics?

Add your teams and players to the FFP app. It is free, and your stream then shows each player with photo, number and stats.

How do we add sponsor logos?

Send your logos as PNG files at least 48 hours before. They go on the scoreboard and full-screen at breaks.

Can we use our own commentator?

Yes. On the 3-camera package they plug a headset into our commentary desk. Or pick one of ours on the Talent page.

What if the match runs over time?

Up to 30 minutes over is included. Longer is charged as an extra hour.

Do we keep the recording?

Yes. The full recording is yours. Download link within 24 hours.

What about several courts or a multi-day tournament?

Use Get an estimate. Tell us the days, courts and schedule, and the estimate is built straight away.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
