Questions

Podcasting

Where do you record?

At your venue: clubhouse, office or event. We bring the full set.

What room do we need?

A quiet room with space for a table and chairs. Our kit runs on battery, so power is a bonus, not a must.

How many people can be on?

Up to 4 hosts and guests, each with their own microphone, filmed from three angles.

How long does set-up take?

About 45 minutes. We arrive early so you start on time.

How many episodes can we record?

2 hours suits one or two episodes. A full day suits a series of four or more.

Do we get video as well as audio?

Yes. You get the audio and all three camera angles.

Can you edit and publish it for us?

Add Edited episode and we deliver files ready for Spotify, Apple Podcasts and YouTube.

Can we stream it live?

Yes. Add Livestream the podcast and it goes live on YouTube or Facebook while you record.

Can you make clips for social media?

Add Social clips for 5 vertical clips of the best moments.

Who owns the recording?

You do. Publish it anywhere.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
