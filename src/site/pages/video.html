Questions

Video

How long until I get my footage?

All raw footage within 48 hours. Edited films within 5 days.

Is the footage edited?

The booking includes all raw footage. Add a Highlights film or Social clips for edited videos.

What format do I get?

4K MP4 files by download link. Social clips come in vertical format for Instagram, TikTok and Reels.

Can you film interviews on the day?

Yes. Add Interviews and we set up a lit, miked spot with your logo backdrop.

Can I ask for changes to the edit?

Yes, one round of changes is included on every edited film.

Do you add music?

Yes. Edited films and clips use licensed music, safe for social media.

Who owns the video?

You do. Post it anywhere, as often as you like.

What should I send before the day?

The schedule, the key moments you want, and any people to interview.

Can you cover two things happening at once?

Add a Second videographer so both are covered.

Do you film with drones?

Drone filming needs a UAE permit. Ask for it in Get an estimate and we quote it.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
