Photography

How many photos do I get?

Every good shot, edited. Usually 150 or more for 2 hours, and 400 or more for a full day.

How fast are the photos ready?

Online gallery within 48 hours. Add Same-day photos to get 30 edited photos the same evening.

How are photos delivered?

A private online gallery. Anyone with the link can view and download.

Can players download their own photos?

Yes. Share the gallery link with the squad.

What size are the photos?

Full resolution, ready for print, posters and social media.

Do you do team and individual portraits?

Yes. Add Team and player portraits and we bring lights and set up a portrait spot.

Can I get the RAW files?

No. You get every photo edited by hand, which is the finished result.

Who owns the photos?

You do. Post and print anywhere.

What should I send before the day?

The schedule and any must-have shots, such as the trophy presentation or sponsors.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
