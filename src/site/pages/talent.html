Questions

Talent

Who are the talent?

Commentators, MCs, presenters, interviewers and announcers with an approved FFP Talent profile. Every profile is checked against a real reel.

Can I watch them first?

Yes. Every profile has a reel of their work.

Can I book talent without your crew?

Yes. Book them on their own for your event, or add them to a livestream.

What is included?

The talent on site for the time you book, plus preparation: team lists, name pronunciation and your run sheet.

What should I send them?

Team sheets, the schedule and any sponsor reads, at least 48 hours before.

Can they work in Arabic?

Filter by language to see who presents in Arabic and other languages.

How do I join as talent?

Create your FFP Talent profile and upload your reel. Once approved, you show here.

Booking and payment

How do I book?

Pick a service, choose 2 hours or a full day, add your date and details, then confirm. Your price shows before you pay.

Do you travel outside Dubai?

Yes, anywhere in the UAE. Dubai is included. Other emirates add a travel charge per crew member per day, from AED 250 for Sharjah and Ajman. It shows before you pay.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Your invoice is emailed as soon as you book.

What if I need to cancel?

More than 7 days before: full refund. 2 to 7 days before: 50% back. Less than 48 hours: no refund.

How do I pay?

In advance, by bank transfer for the full invoice amount. Over AED 10,000, pay 50% to book and the rest before the work starts.

Who do you work with?

Clubs, schools, academies, federations and event organisers, in Dubai and across the UAE.

Can I change the date?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before your event.
